# ImageGen prompt

Built-in ImageGen was used with the `ui-mockup` use case.

```text
Design a high-fidelity 1536×1024 desktop application mockup for a unified video, audio, and subtitle timeline editor. Preserve a cool-neutral near-black dark theme and compact Codex/DaVinci-style editing density. Show the full app shell, a persistent left conversation/canvas sidebar, a central 16:9 preview and sparse transport controls, then a fixed-header timeline with one sequential purple video track, attached embedded-audio waveform strips inside each video clip, two independent green audio tracks, one amber subtitle track, a blue playhead, horizontal scroll and zoom. Show a selected subtitle cue and a compact right inspector with cue text, integer-millisecond timing, Saved state, and CAS hash. Use existing semantic colors: primary blue, video purple, audio green, restrained amber subtitles. Use 1px borders, 6px radius, Inter/system typography, no gradients or oversized cards. Do not show overlays, picture-in-picture, transitions, speed controls, keyframes, ducking, auto-transcription, effects, or color grading.
```
